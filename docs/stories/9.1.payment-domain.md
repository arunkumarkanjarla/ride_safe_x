# Story 9.1 — Payment domain & sandbox gateway

Status: Ready for Review · Phase 9

## Policy (Phase 9 sandbox)
- Eligible booking status: `CLOSED` only (post-trip fare settlement).
- Amount/currency from booking `estimate_paise` / `currency` (server).
- At most one `SUCCEEDED` payment per booking.
- Payment status independent of trip lifecycle.

## States
CREATED → PENDING → {SUCCEEDED | FAILED | CANCELLED}
CREATED → CANCELLED
PENDING → EXPIRED (reserved; not auto-expired in Phase 9 UI)

Terminal: SUCCEEDED, FAILED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED (no regress).
