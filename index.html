RideNova

Rides, long trips, food and parcels: customer, driver and partner apps, the admin portal and the agent portal.

Customer appSign up, book rides and long trips, order food, send parcels, savings, wallet and help.Driver appOnboarding and documents, going online, ride and delivery jobs, earnings and payouts.Partner appRestaurant orders, hotel, rental and bus bookings, menus, payouts and reviews.Admin portalOperations, dispatch, driver review, pricing, refunds, safety, fraud, reports and roles.Agent portalWeb portal for RideNova agents: calls, e-mail, chat and social media from customers, drivers and partners. Controlled by the admin portal.
