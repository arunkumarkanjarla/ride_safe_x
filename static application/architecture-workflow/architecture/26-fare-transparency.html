Fare transparency & daily driver fee

Drivers keep 100% of every fare. Instead of taking a cut of each ride, the platform charges drivers one small flat fee per working day: ₹10 to ₹15 by zone, and nothing on days with no rides. Riders and drivers can both see where every rupee goes, including tax and what the daily fee pays for (servers, payment gateway, social media, support). Nothing is ever added on top of the price shown.

Rules

How the daily fee works

  • No ride, no fee. The fee is charged only on days the driver completes at least one ride or delivery. Days offline or with no trips cost nothing.
  • One flat amount per day: ₹10 to ₹15 depending on the zone. It does not grow with the number of rides or the money earned.
  • Never more than 10% of the day's fares. One ₹50 ride pays ₹4.76, not ₹15.
  • Worked in more than one zone? The driver pays the lowest of those zones' fees.
  • One fee covers everything that day: city rides, long trips, food and parcel deliveries.
  • Taken at midnight from the driver wallet. If the wallet is empty it is taken from the next earnings. No interest, no late fee. After 3 unpaid days the driver is asked to clear it before going online.
  • GST invoice every month for all daily fees paid, so drivers can see and claim them.

Promise

No hidden charges: the rules the system enforces

  • One price for riders. The amount on the booking button is the amount charged, GST included. No booking fee, convenience fee, card surcharge or platform charge.
  • 100% of the fare goes to the driver. The platform takes nothing from any ride: not from the fare, tips, tolls, parking or surge.
  • Same numbers for both sides. The rider's receipt and the driver's trip summary come from one stored record and match to the paisa.
  • Lines must add up. Driver + GST + pass-through = rider total, and the daily fee's uses add up to the fee. The ledger refuses anything that does not reconcile.
  • Server, payment gateway, social media and support costs are paid from the daily fee, never from the rider's price or the driver's fare.
  • Coupons are paid by the platform. The driver is paid the full fare before the coupon.
  • Fee changes are public and announced on a public page, reaching drivers 7 days before they apply, with a hard maximum of ₹15 a day.
