Checks

Validation and safety rules

  • Mock location: Android mock-location flag and impossible jumps (over 150 km/h between points) mark the point as suspect and notify Fraud.
  • Route deviation: distance from the planned polyline over 500 m for more than 2 minutes raises a safety check to the rider.
  • Long stop: no movement for 5 minutes on a trip (3 at night) asks both parties if all is well.
