Resilience

Graceful degradation

If this failsThe platform keeps working by
Maps providerSwitching to the backup provider; if both fail, straight-line estimates with a 'fare may change' note.
Payment gatewayOffering cash or wallet; failed online payments settled later.
SMS providerFalling back to a second SMS provider or WhatsApp for OTP.
KafkaServices keep writing to the outbox; events flow once Kafka is back, nothing is lost.
RedisDispatch pauses new matches for a few seconds while the replica is promoted; running trips are unaffected.
AI modelsRules-based fallbacks: fixed surge caps, simple ETA, fraud rules only.
TelephonyIn-app chat stays available; calls fall back to a second SIP trunk.
