Sharing seats and parcels

Safety

Trust and safety rules

  • Drivers and riders are ID-verified; ratings are shown for long trips separately from city rides.
  • Women-only seats can be reserved and only booked by women who chose that option.
  • Live trip sharing, SOS and auto check-in work the same as city rides, including offline.
  • Parcels: contents declared, prohibited items list per country, photo and code at every handover, value limit without insurance.
  • Drivers can refuse a parcel that looks different from its declaration; the platform supports them.
  • Local law decides whether private cars may charge more than cost sharing; pricing enforces it.
