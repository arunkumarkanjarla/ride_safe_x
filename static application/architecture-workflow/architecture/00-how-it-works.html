Step by step

What each person sees and what happens behind it

StepRiderDriverWhat the platform does
1. Sign upPhone + OTP login, profile, emergency contacts
R03 Login with phone, R04 Verify OTP		Register, upload licence, RC, insurance, permit
D02 Registration, D03 Upload documents		Auth issues JWT + refresh token. Driver documents go to MinIO and into the KYC review queue.
2. Driver goes online—Toggles Online after checks pass
D06 Online & waiting		Driver position written to the Redis geo index every few seconds. Heat map of demand shown to the driver.
3. Rider picks a destinationSearches place, sees fares per vehicle
R08 Search destination, R09 Ride options & fare		—Maps adapter returns route, distance and ETA. Pricing applies base fare, per-km, per-minute and zone surge.
4. BookingConfirms pickup and payment method
R10 Confirm pickup & pay, R11 Finding driver		—Ride created as REQUESTED with an idempotency key. Event ride.requested published.
5. MatchingSees search animationGets an offer with pickup, drop and fare
D07 Incoming ride request		Dispatch searches nearby drivers, filters and scores them, locks one driver and sends a 15 s offer.
6. Driver on the wayDriver photo, car, plate, OTP, live ETA
R12 Driver assigned		Navigates to pickup
D08 Navigate to pickup		Ride becomes DRIVER_ACCEPTED. Location streamed to the rider over WebSocket.
7. ContactMasked call or chat
R13 Masked call, R14 In-ride chat		Same, from the trip screenTelephony bridges the call through Asterisk with a proxy number so neither side sees the real number.
8. PickupTells the driver the OTPEnters OTP, trip starts
D09 Arrived & start trip		OTP checked; ride becomes TRIP_STARTED.
9. On the tripLive tracking, share trip, SOS
R15 Trip in progress, R16 SOS emergency		Turn-by-turn navigation
D10 Trip navigation		Location service checks route deviation and long stops; Safety service opens an incident on SOS.
10. DropFare breakdown, pay
R17 Trip complete & payment		Collects cash or sees online payment
D11 Trip complete & collect		TRIP_COMPLETED. Payment captured, 100% of the fare credited to the driver, invoice generated. No commission; one daily fee at midnight.
11. RatingsR18 Rate driverD12 Rate riderRatings stored; fraud and safety models consume the completed-ride event.
12. After the rideHistory, invoice, support
R19 Invoice, R20 Ride history, R26 Support & AI assistant		Earnings, payouts
D13 Earnings, D14 Wallet & payouts		Ops team sees everything in A02 Operations command center and A04 Ride detail & timeline; audit log keeps every step.
