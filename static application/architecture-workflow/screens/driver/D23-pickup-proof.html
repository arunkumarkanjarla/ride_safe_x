How it works

  1. Each step must be completed in order; the slider unlocks after the photo.
  2. Photos are compressed on the phone and uploaded when there is network.
  3. Delivery is confirmed by the customer's OTP or, if allowed, a doorstep photo with location.
  4. Parcels show the sender's declaration; partners can refuse suspicious items.

What is on the screen

  • Step list
  • Camera capture
  • Rules for this job
  • Slide to confirm pickup

Edge cases and validation

  • Seal broken: partner reports it before leaving; restaurant remakes or order is cancelled.
  • Customer unreachable for 10 min: support decides return or leave at door.
