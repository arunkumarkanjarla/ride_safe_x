Driver & delivery app · D11

Trip complete & collect

Shows what the rider paid, the tax and what the driver earned: 100% of the fare, with no commission.

How it works

  1. Final fare comes from the server after end trip.
  2. Online payments need no action; cash rides need 'Cash received'.
  3. Earnings post to the driver ledger when payment completes.
  4. Cash rides show 'Collect ₹248'; only the GST is settled from the wallet.

Edge cases and validation

  • Rider disputes cash: support can check the confirmation time and GPS.
  • Coupon discounts are paid by the platform, never taken from the driver.
  • The daily fee is never shown per trip; it is charged once a day (D27).
