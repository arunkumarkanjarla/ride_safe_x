How it works

  1. SOS works like the rider SOS and opens a P1 incident.
  2. Accident flow asks if anyone is hurt and shows first steps.
  3. Breakdown ends the trip safely and re-dispatches the rider.

What is on the screen

  • Hold-to-send SOS
  • Situation shortcuts
  • Emergency call
