How it works

  1. Waiting timer starts at 'arrived'.
  2. The OTP is checked on the server; three wrong tries alert Safety.
  3. Correct OTP sets TRIP_STARTED and switches to trip navigation.

Edge cases and validation

  • No-show after 7 min: cancel with fee to the driver.
  • OTP entry works offline and syncs later if the trip already started.
