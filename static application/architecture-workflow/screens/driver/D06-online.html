How it works

  1. Location is sent every 10 s while idle.
  2. Demand heat map refreshes every 2 minutes from the demand model.
  3. Offers arrive over WebSocket with a push notification as backup.
  4. Driver can set a destination filter to get rides towards home.

What is on the screen

  • Online status
  • Demand heat map
  • Today's earnings, trips, hours
  • Surge hint
  • Quest progress
  • Go offline

Edge cases and validation

  • Network drops: app keeps the online state for 60 s, then marks offline on the server.
  • Driver online 12 hours: forced break for rest, as many regulations require.
