RULES THAT APPLY
• Parcels need a 5-minute safety training (done ✓)• Food on a ride only when no passenger is on board• Long trips need 2 hours rest in every 10 hours

How it works

  1. Each service has its own requirements (vehicle type, training, insulated bag for food).
  2. Dispatch only offers jobs from enabled services.
  3. Rules prevent unsafe or illegal combinations, such as food pickups with a passenger on board.
  4. Earnings and incentives are tracked per service.

What is on the screen

  • Service toggles with details
  • Applicable rules
  • Save and go online
