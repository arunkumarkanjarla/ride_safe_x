How it works

  1. Opened from Trip complete (D11), Earnings (D13) or Wallet (D14).
  2. Before midnight it shows the fee that will be charged; after midnight, the fee that was charged.
  3. No completed rides: the fee is ₹0. A slow day: the fee is capped at 10% of the day's fares.
  4. Worked in several zones: the lowest zone fee applies; the screen says which one.

What is on the screen

  • What the driver keeps today
  • Riders paid, GST, fares
  • Daily fee and what it pays for
  • Saving vs commission
  • Daily fee in each zone

Edge cases and validation

  • Fee change coming: a banner shows the new amount and the date it applies, at least 7 days ahead.
  • Wallet empty at midnight: the fee is taken from the next earnings, with no interest or late fee.
  • 3 unpaid days: the driver is asked to clear them before going online.
  • Wrong fee: 'Raise with support' attaches the day's ride list automatically.
