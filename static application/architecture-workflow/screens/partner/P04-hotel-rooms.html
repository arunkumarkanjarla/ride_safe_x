How it works

  1. Availability and rates can be edited here or synced from a channel manager.
  2. Guests booked with transport show their expected arrival time from the trip plan.
  3. Payouts follow the hotel's policy: pay at hotel or prepaid.

What is on the screen

  • 7-day availability and price strip
  • Channel sync status
  • Today's arrivals with ETA
  • Month summary

Backend calls

MethodEndpoint / channelWhy
PUT/v1/stays/{id}/ratesRates and availability
GET/v1/stays/{id}/arrivalsArrivals

Events published

stay.rates_updated

Data used

  • stays.rates, stays.bookings

Edge cases and validation

  • Overbooking from another channel: the system closes sales here immediately and alerts the hotel.
  • Guest bus delayed: arrival time updates automatically.
