How it works

  1. Each vehicle has a calendar; bookings from the app block it automatically.
  2. Handover and return follow a photo checklist that both sides see.
  3. Deposits are held by the platform and released or partly charged after return.

What is on the screen

  • Fleet timeline for the week
  • Legend
  • Today's handover with photo checklist

Edge cases and validation

  • Damage claims need matching photos from handover and return.
  • Vehicle marked in service hides it from search for those dates.
