How it works

  1. Operators connect their reservation system by API, or manage seats here directly.
  2. Seats sold in the app are pushed to the operator system instantly to avoid double sale.
  3. Conductors check passengers in by scanning the ticket QR, which also works offline.
  4. A GPS device or the conductor's phone shares live bus location.

What is on the screen

  • Sync status
  • Today's trips with seats sold and status
  • Boarding list with check-in
  • Parcels on board

Data used

Edge cases and validation

  • Bus breakdown: operator marks it; passengers are notified and offered the next bus or a refund.
  • Seat sync failure stops sales for that trip until fixed.
