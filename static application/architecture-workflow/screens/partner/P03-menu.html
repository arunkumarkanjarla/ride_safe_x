How it works

  1. Toggling an item updates the customer menu within seconds.
  2. Price band is calculated from average order value; partners can't pick it.
  3. Time-limited items appear only in their window.
  4. Partner-funded offers are shown separately from platform offers.

What is on the screen

  • Price band
  • Items with in-stock toggles
  • Opening hours
  • Offers
  • Pause orders
