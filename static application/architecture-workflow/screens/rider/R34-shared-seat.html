How it works

  1. Drivers publish long trips with seats, price per seat and pickup points (see D24).
  2. Riders pick a seat; women-only seats are bookable only by women riders who chose that option.
  3. Pickup points are fixed stops on the route so the driver doesn't make long detours.
  4. Payment is held and released to the driver after the trip.

What is on the screen

  • Driver profile and verification
  • Seat map with women-only seats
  • Co-travellers
  • Pickup points with times
  • Price breakdown vs private cab
  • Book seat

Edge cases and validation

  • Seat prices are capped to cost sharing where carpool laws require it.
  • Driver cancels: riders get an automatic rebooking offer and a full refund.
  • Co-traveller details are shown only as first name, rating and verified status.
