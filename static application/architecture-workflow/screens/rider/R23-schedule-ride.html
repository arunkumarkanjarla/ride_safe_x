A driver is assigned 15 minutes before pickup. Free cancellation until then.

How it works

  1. Rider picks date, time, route and vehicle; the app shows an estimated fare range.
  2. The scheduler starts dispatch 20 minutes before pickup.
  3. Rider is notified when a driver is assigned.

What is on the screen

  • Date chips
  • Time picker in 15-minute steps
  • Pickup and drop
  • Vehicle with fare range
  • Cancellation policy
  • Schedule button

Edge cases and validation

  • If no driver is found 5 minutes before pickup, the rider is told immediately with options.
  • Airport rides allow flight number for delay tracking later.
