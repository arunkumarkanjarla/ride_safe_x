How it works

  1. App subscribes to the ride channel over WebSocket (push as backup).
  2. Dispatch offers the ride to one driver at a time, 15 s each, widening the radius.
  3. As soon as a driver accepts, the screen switches to driver assigned.
  4. After 3 minutes the rider sees 'No drivers available' with options.

What is on the screen

  • Pulsing radar around pickup
  • Progress and message
  • Trip summary
  • Fare and payment
  • Cancel (free at this stage)

Edge cases and validation

  • No driver found: suggest another vehicle type or retry with a small fare boost.
  • App killed during search: on reopen, resumes this screen.
  • Network lost: status resyncs from the API when reconnected.
