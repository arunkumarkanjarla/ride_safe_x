How it works

  1. Rates and policies come live from the partner when the page opens.
  2. Room is held for 10 minutes while paying.
  3. Voucher is saved in Trips & tickets and works offline.

What is on the screen

  • Photo
  • Name, tier, distance, rating
  • Amenities
  • Room options with policy
  • Price breakdown with bundle saving
  • Add to trip

Edge cases and validation

  • Partner overbooking: rebooked at equal or better tier at no extra cost.
