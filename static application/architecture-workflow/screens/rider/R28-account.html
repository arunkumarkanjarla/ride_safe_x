How it works

  1. Each row opens its settings screen.
  2. Privacy & data lets the rider download their data or request deletion (handled within the legal time limit).
  3. Log out revokes the refresh token on the server.

What is on the screen

  • Profile summary and rider rating
  • Settings list
  • Log out

  • Unpaid dues must be settled before deletion.
