How it works

  1. Location updates every 3 s; the route redraws if the driver takes another road.
  2. If the car goes off route or stops too long, the app asks 'Is everything OK?'.
  3. Share trip sends a live link to chosen contacts.
  4. SOS opens the emergency screen.

What is on the screen

  • Live route and car
  • ETA and remaining distance
  • Progress bar
  • Driver row with call
  • Change destination
  • Share trip, safety toolkit
  • SOS button

Edge cases and validation

  • Destination change shows the new fare before confirming.
  • Deviation check-in unanswered for 60 s: safety team is alerted.
  • Phone goes offline: ride continues; driver app is the source of truth.
