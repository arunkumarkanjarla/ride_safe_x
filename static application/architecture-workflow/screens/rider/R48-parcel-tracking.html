How it works

  1. Each handover needs the next person to scan the parcel code and take a photo.
  2. Location follows the carrier's trip; dead zones are shown as expected gaps.
  3. The receiver gets a tracking link and an OTP without installing the app.
  4. Delivery proof is the receiver's OTP and a photo.

What is on the screen

  • Carrier and ETA
  • Receiver OTP
  • Handover steps
  • Proof photos
  • Call carrier and share tracking
