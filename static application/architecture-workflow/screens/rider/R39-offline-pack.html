How it works

  1. Map tiles, tickets, contacts and emergency numbers for the route's regions are saved encrypted on the phone.
  2. Payments for bookings in the dead zone are taken in advance where possible.
  3. Auto check-in: the server expects the phone back online by the end of the stretch plus a buffer; if not, contacts and the safety team are alerted.
  4. Backup rules are stored with the trip so rebooking needs no network.

  • Pack expires 48 hours after the trip and deletes itself.
