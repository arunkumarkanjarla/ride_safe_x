How it works

  1. Restaurant status updates arrive from the partner app.
  2. Delivery partner location streams like a ride.
  3. Batched orders show how many stops are before yours.
  4. OTP or a doorstep photo confirms delivery.

What is on the screen

  • Map with route
  • ETA and delivery type
  • Order steps
  • Delivery partner with masked call
  • Delivery OTP
