How it works

  1. Loads 20 rides at a time with cursor pagination.
  2. Tapping a ride shows its receipt, route map and help options.
  3. Scheduled rides can be edited or cancelled here.

What is on the screen

  • Filter chips
  • Ride rows with date, route, fare, status
  • Bottom navigation

Edge cases and validation

  • Rides older than the retention period show summary only.
  • Offline: shows the last cached page.
