How it works

  1. Every booking from every service writes to one bookings index.
  2. Tickets, QR codes and vouchers are cached encrypted on the phone.
  3. Plans are grouped so a multi-leg trip shows as one item.

What is on the screen

  • Upcoming / past
  • Trip card with offline QR
  • Other legs of the plan
  • Recent orders and parcels
  • Bottom navigation

Backend calls

MethodEndpoint / channelWhy
GET/v1/bookings?status=upcomingBookings across services

Events published

None

Data used

  • bookings index
  • Local encrypted cache

Edge cases and validation

  • Ticket changed by operator: the cached QR is replaced and the user notified.
  • Past bookings older than retention show summary only.
