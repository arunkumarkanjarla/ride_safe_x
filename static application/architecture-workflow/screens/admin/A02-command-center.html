How it works

  1. KPIs stream over WebSocket every 5 s from aggregated Kafka metrics.
  2. Map shows driver positions (sampled), demand heat and surge zones.
  3. Safety alerts sort by age; an unassigned SOS over 45 s turns the tile red and pages the safety lead.
  4. Every tile links to the detailed screen.

What is on the screen

  • KPI row with deltas
  • Live city map with heat and surge zones
  • Safety alerts list
  • Work queues needing attention
  • Rides per hour chart
  • City health table

Edge cases and validation

  • If live data is older than 30 s, a grey 'stale' label appears on each tile.
  • Views are filtered by the user's cities and role.
