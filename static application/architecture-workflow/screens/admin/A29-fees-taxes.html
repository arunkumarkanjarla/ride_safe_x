How it works

  1. Each zone has a versioned schedule of fares and a daily fee (₹10–15); the system refuses anything above ₹15.
  2. A change is a draft until a second approver signs; drivers are notified 7 days before it applies and the public fee page updates.
  3. The midnight job charges one fee per driver who completed at least one trip, using the lowest zone they worked in and the 10% cap.
  4. A nightly check confirms every ride's lines sum to what the rider paid and that no driver was charged twice; mismatches alert finance.

What is on the screen

  • Money KPIs including drivers charged, capped and not charged
  • Zone fares and daily fees
  • Pending change with notice date
  • Cost per driver-day
  • Propose, history, simulate

Edge cases and validation

  • A daily fee above ₹15, or a per-ride commission, can't be configured.
  • A change can't apply sooner than 7 days after drivers are told.
  • If costs exceed fees in a zone, the loss stays with the platform; fares and fees can't be raised retroactively.
