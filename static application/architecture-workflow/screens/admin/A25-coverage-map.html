Sources: anonymous signal readings from apps (opt-in), driver reports, carrier data where licensed.

How it works

  1. Apps record signal strength and network type with location (opt-in, anonymous) and upload in batches.
  2. Readings are aggregated into map cells per network; cells with no signal over many readings become dead zones.
  3. Dead zones are joined with roads to produce stretches with length and confidence.
  4. Ops can add road restrictions such as night closures.

Data used

  • coverage.readings (raw, 30 days)
  • coverage.tiles (aggregated)
  • road_rules

Edge cases and validation

  • Raw readings are deleted after 30 days; only aggregates are kept.
  • Zones with few readings are marked low confidence.
