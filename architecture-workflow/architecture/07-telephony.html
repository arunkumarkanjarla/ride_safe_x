Number masking and recording

RuleDetail
Proxy bindingA proxy number is bound to the rider–driver pair from DRIVER_ACCEPTED until 30 minutes after the trip ends.
Pool sizeReuse is possible because a proxy is resolved by caller number + proxy number, so a small pool serves many rides.
After binding expiresCalls to the proxy reach the support IVR instead of the other party.
RecordingOnly where the law allows, with an announcement at call start; stored encrypted with a retention period set per country.
Abuse limitsMax calls per ride and per hour; repeated late-night calls after the trip trigger a safety review.
FallbackIf WebRTC fails on a weak network, the app falls back to a phone call through the proxy number.
