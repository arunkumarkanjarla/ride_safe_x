Illustrative only. There is no per-ride commission: the driver is credited the whole fare and one flat daily fee is posted at midnight (see page 26). Tax rules vary by country and vehicle type; they are configuration, not code.

Payment states

Payment stateMeaning
CREATEDOrder created at the gateway, rider hasn't paid yet.
AUTHORISEDCard held or UPI mandate approved; nothing captured.
CAPTUREDMoney taken; waiting for settlement to the platform account.
FAILEDDeclined, timed out or cancelled by the rider.
REFUND_PENDING → REFUNDEDFull or partial refund requested by support or policy.
SETTLEDGateway has paid the platform; reconciliation matched.
