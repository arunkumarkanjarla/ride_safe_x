Guardrails

Fairness rules

  • Coupons never reduce what drivers and partners earn unless the partner agreed to fund the offer.
  • Each campaign has a hard budget; redemptions stop when it's spent.
  • Fraud rules block multiple accounts on one device or payment method from farming coupons.
  • The final price on the booking button is the price charged, including taxes and fees.
