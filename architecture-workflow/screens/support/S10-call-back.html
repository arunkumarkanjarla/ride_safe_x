Call me back

Voice support: request a call-back and follow queue, ringing, connected and ended states (simulated). New application.

Call me back
Simulated voice support · no call is made, no microphone is used.
Average wait 2 minAn agent calls you back from a masked number.
HelpTicketsChatCall

How it works

  1. Choose the topic and confirm the request.
  2. Queue position updates, then the phone rings: Answer or Decline.
  3. Connected shows a call timer; Hang up shows a summary.
  4. Busy, missed and failed outcomes offer a retry or a ticket.

What is on the screen

  • Simulation notice
  • Topic
  • Masked number
  • Call status card
  • Request / Answer / Decline / Hang up
  • Summary
