How it works

  1. Shows the conversation and status.
  2. Replying to a 'Waiting on you' ticket moves it back to the agent.
  3. Closed tickets can be reopened with a reason; resolved tickets can be rated.

What is on the screen

  • Status and assignee
  • Conversation
  • Reply box
  • Send
  • Attach (simulated)
  • Close / reopen
  • Rate support

Edge cases and validation

  • Empty or over-long messages are refused.
  • Messages cannot be added to a closed ticket.
