Actions

How it works

  1. Assign to me starts work; Reassign moves the case to another agent.
  2. Replies go to the customer; internal notes stay in the console.
  3. Resolve needs a category and notes; Close and Reopen follow the ticket state machine.
  4. Every change is added to the case history.

What is on the screen

  • Customer context
  • Reference
  • Conversation
  • Reply and internal note
  • Assign/reassign
  • Priority
  • Escalate
  • Resolution
  • Close/reopen
  • History
