How it works

  1. Manifest updates as bookings arrive; it is cached offline before departure.
  2. Each passenger and parcel is checked in with OTP or code scan.
  3. Parcel drops are added to navigation as stops.
  4. Earnings show the full fare; there is no per-trip fee.

What is on the screen

  • Seat, parcel and earning summary
  • Passengers with pickup and payment status
  • Parcels with drop points
  • Offline pack status

Edge cases and validation

  • No-show passenger: seat payment goes to the driver after 10 min wait.
  • Codes work offline: they are verified against the cached manifest and synced later.
