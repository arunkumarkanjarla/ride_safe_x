How it works

  1. Numbers come from the ledger, grouped by day in the city's time zone.
  2. Tapping a bar lists that day's trips.
  3. Week, month and custom ranges are available.

What is on the screen

  • Period selector
  • Total
  • Daily bar chart
  • Trips, hours, tips
  • Breakdown by source

Edge cases and validation

  • Pending payments are shown separately until settled.
  • Daily fees are shown as their own line so totals are honest; days without rides show ₹0.
