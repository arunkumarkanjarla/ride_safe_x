Driver & delivery app · D26

No-network stretch ahead

Warns the driver before a no-network stretch and confirms the trip will keep working offline.

Open D26 in the Driver & delivery app prototype The mock below is the approved design; the working screen is in the application.

How it works

  1. The coverage service has flagged the stretch on this route; the warning appears 4 km before it.
  2. All trip data is already cached; the app switches to offline mode automatically.
  3. Fuel and rest suggestions come from the route places service.
  4. A check-in window is set on the server for the expected exit time.

What is on the screen

  • No-network banner with length and time
  • Offline readiness list
  • Last fuel stop
  • Road restriction
  • Auto check-in note

Edge cases and validation

  • If the car stops for more than 15 min inside the stretch, the app asks the driver to send an SMS check-in at the next signal.
  • SOS is queued and sent by SMS as soon as any signal appears.
