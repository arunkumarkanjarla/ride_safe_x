How it works

  1. Going online runs checks: documents valid, no blocks, selfie match for the shift, vehicle selected.
  2. If a check fails, the slider explains what to fix.
  3. Once online, location sharing starts and the driver can receive offers.

What is on the screen

  • Driver card with rating and plate
  • Offline status
  • Document warning
  • Today and weekly summary
  • Slide to go online
  • Bottom navigation

Edge cases and validation

  • Expired mandatory document: going online is blocked.
  • Background location permission required on Android to receive rides with the screen off.
