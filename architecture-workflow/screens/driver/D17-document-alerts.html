How it works

  1. A nightly job checks expiry dates: reminders at 30, 15, 7 and 1 day.
  2. On expiry of a mandatory document the driver can't go online.
  3. A new upload goes to KYC review; old document stays until approved.

What is on the screen

  • Blocking banner
  • Document list with expiry and status
  • Upload button

Edge cases and validation

  • Expiry during an online shift: the current trip finishes, then the driver is taken offline.
  • Which documents are mandatory is set per city.
