In places where carpool laws cap prices to cost sharing, the maximum is applied automatically.

How it works

  1. Route, time and pickup points create a sharing trip.
  2. Suggested price is based on fuel and tolls for the route divided by seats, within local rules.
  3. Parcels are matched by size and drop point along the way.
  4. Bookings arrive in the manifest (D25).

Edge cases and validation

  • Commercial vehicles and private cars follow different rules per country; the form adapts.
  • Trips with no bookings 2 hours before leaving can be cancelled without penalty.
