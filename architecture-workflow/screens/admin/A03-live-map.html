How it works

  1. Positions stream from the location service; at city scale the map clusters markers.
  2. Filters change the WebSocket subscription on the server, not just what is drawn.
  3. Clicking a car opens the driver card; clicking a trip draws its route and trail.

What is on the screen

  • Status filters
  • City and vehicle selectors
  • Legend with counts
  • Map with cars, heat and zones
  • Selected driver panel with actions

Backend calls

MethodEndpoint / channelWhy

None

Data used

  • Redis GEO
  • Location stream

Edge cases and validation

  • Exact rider locations are only shown to roles with ride.view.
  • Map falls back to 10 s polling if WebSocket is blocked by a proxy.
