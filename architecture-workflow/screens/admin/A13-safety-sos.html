How it works

  1. New SOS alerts ring an alarm and appear at the top; unassigned after 45 s pages the safety lead.
  2. Agent assigns, calls both parties through masked lines and notes each step.
  3. Contact with police or ambulance is recorded as an action with the reference number.
  4. Closing requires a category and summary; evidence is frozen automatically.

What is on the screen

  • Safety KPIs
  • Incident list with age and owner
  • Live map of the ride
  • Actions, notes and incident timeline
