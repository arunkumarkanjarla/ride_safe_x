How it works

  1. Counts update every few seconds from order events.
  2. Late orders are flagged with the main cause from the timeline.
  3. Ops can credit customers, call partners or reassign delivery.

What is on the screen

  • Order stage counts
  • Late orders with reasons and actions
  • Restaurants needing attention
  • Orders over time
