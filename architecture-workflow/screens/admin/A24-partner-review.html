How it works

  1. Applications from the partner app enter this queue.
  2. Registration, tax ID, bank account and location are checked automatically where APIs exist.
  3. Price band is computed, not chosen by the partner.
  4. Approval activates the partner's listing in the selected cities.

What is on the screen

  • Queue by type and age
  • Documents and photos
  • Check results
  • Approve / ask / reject
