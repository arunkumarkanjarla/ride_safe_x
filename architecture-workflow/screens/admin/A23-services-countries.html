How it works

  1. A country pack holds everything local: currency, languages, payments, tax, emergency numbers, document rules, map provider and legal limits.
  2. Services move through Planned → Pilot → Live per city.
  3. Launching a country is a checklist with owners, so nothing is forgotten.
  4. Apps read the matrix to decide what to show.

What is on the screen

  • Global KPIs
  • Country × service matrix
  • Country pack draft
  • Launch checklist with owners

Edge cases and validation

  • A service can't go Live while required pack items are missing.
  • Turning a service off finishes active bookings first.
