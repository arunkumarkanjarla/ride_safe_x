How it works

  1. Reports query the analytics store, never the live database.
  2. Saved reports can be scheduled and emailed as CSV or PDF.
  3. Exports containing personal data need approval and expire after 30 days.

What is on the screen

  • Filters
  • Bookings chart
  • Saved and scheduled reports
  • Cancellation analysis table
  • Export
