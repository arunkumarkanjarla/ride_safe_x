How it works

  1. Summary, route and timeline load from the ride, location and event services.
  2. The timeline is built from ride_events, dispatch, calls and safety events.
  3. Actions require the matching permission and are written to the audit log with a reason.

What is on the screen

  • Status chip
  • Parties with masked phone numbers
  • Fare, payment, surge
  • Route and GPS trail map
  • Event timeline
  • Actions: call, refund, cancel, open incident

Edge cases and validation

  • Full phone numbers only for roles with user.pii.view_full, and viewing is audited.
  • Refunds above the limit require a second approver.
