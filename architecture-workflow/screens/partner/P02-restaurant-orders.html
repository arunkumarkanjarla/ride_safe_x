How it works

  1. New orders ring until accepted; unaccepted orders auto-reject after 3 minutes.
  2. The chosen prep time tells dispatch when to send the partner.
  3. Order states update the customer's tracking screen.
  4. Restaurants can pause orders when the kitchen is overloaded.

What is on the screen

  • Accepting status
  • New order with items, note and prep time
  • Preparing list
  • Ready list

Edge cases and validation

  • Poor internet at the restaurant: orders also arrive by SMS and a call.
  • Frequent rejections lower ranking and trigger a call from the partner team.
