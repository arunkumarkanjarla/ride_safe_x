How it works

  1. Earnings are computed from the ledger with a line for each deduction.
  2. Payout schedule depends on partner type and country.
  3. Partners can reply to reviews once; replies are public.

What is on the screen

  • Next payout
  • Breakdown of deductions
  • Reviews with reply
