How it works

  1. Partner picks a type; the document list comes from the country pack for that type.
  2. Applications go to the admin review queue (A24).
  3. Once approved, the partner sees the tools for their type.
  4. One partner account can own several outlets or properties.

Edge cases and validation

  • Food partners need a food safety licence where the law requires it (for example FSSAI in India).
  • Duplicate businesses are detected by tax ID and location.
