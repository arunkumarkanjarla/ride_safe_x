How it works

  1. Stars change the chips: 5 shows compliments, 3 or below shows problems.
  2. 1 or 2 stars with a safety problem opens a ticket automatically.
  3. Tips are charged with the same payment method and paid out in full.

What is on the screen

  • Driver photo
  • Star rating
  • Compliment / problem chips
  • Comment
  • Tip options
  • Submit and skip

Backend calls

MethodEndpoint / channelWhy

Data used

Edge cases and validation

  • Ratings can be changed for 7 days.
  • Drivers see only the average, never who gave a rating.
  • A low rating on a safety topic creates a support ticket.
