How it works

  1. The assistant calls tools like get_ride and get_fare_breakdown for the rider's own rides.
  2. It answers with real numbers or creates a ticket.
  3. Safety topics and refunds above a limit always go to a human agent.
  4. The chat history is attached to the ticket so the rider doesn't repeat themselves.
