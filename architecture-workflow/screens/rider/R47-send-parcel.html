How it works

  1. City parcels use bike or car delivery partners like a ride.
  2. Intercity parcels can ride with a verified traveller on a shared trip, with a bus operator, or by a dedicated courier.
  3. Contents must be declared; prohibited items are listed per country.
  4. Sender and receiver get OTPs; photos are taken at each handover.

What is on the screen

  • City or intercity
  • Sender and receiver
  • Size
  • Delivery options with time and price
  • Contents declaration
  • Book pickup

Edge cases and validation

  • High-value items need a dedicated courier with insurance.
  • Traveller trip cancelled: parcel moves to the next trip or bus at no extra cost.
