How it works

  1. App asks Telephony for a call session; the call is placed over WebRTC to Asterisk.
  2. Asterisk rings the driver app, or the driver's phone through a proxy number.
  3. If WebRTC fails, the app dials the proxy number over the normal phone network.
  4. Call ends; duration saved to call logs.

What is on the screen

  • Driver name and photo
  • Privacy note
  • Timer
  • Mute, speaker, keypad
  • End call
  • Recording notice

Edge cases and validation

  • Masked calling available from driver assigned until 30 min after the trip.
  • Poor network: automatic fallback to a phone call.
  • Driver doesn't answer: suggest chat.
