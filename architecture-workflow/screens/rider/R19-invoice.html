How it works

  1. A Celery worker renders the PDF after payment and stores it in the invoices bucket.
  2. The app opens a signed link valid for 5 minutes.
  3. Business riders get GST/VAT details of their company.

What is on the screen

  • Invoice number and date
  • Route and times
  • Fare and taxes
  • Driver, vehicle, tax ID
  • Download and email

Backend calls

MethodEndpoint / channelWhy
GET/v1/rides/{id}/invoiceSigned PDF link
POST/v1/rides/{id}/invoice/emailEmail invoice

Events published

None

Data used

  • MinIO invoices bucket
  • ledger entries

Edge cases and validation

  • Invoice not ready yet: shows 'Generating…' and a push when ready.
  • Tax format differs per country (GST, VAT).
