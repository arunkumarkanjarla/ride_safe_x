How it works

  1. Restaurants are partners (P02, P03); price band uses average cost for two.
  2. Saver allows batching two nearby orders on one delivery partner, so delivery is free or cheaper.
  3. Delivery time estimate = restaurant prep time + partner travel, learned per restaurant.
  4. Offers shown are the best the user can actually use.

What is on the screen

  • Delivery address
  • Priority chips
  • Price band and quick filters
  • Restaurant cards with price for two, time, rating, offer
  • Saver explanation
