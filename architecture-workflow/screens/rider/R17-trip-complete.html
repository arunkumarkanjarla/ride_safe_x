How it works

  1. Ride service computes the final fare from the snapped GPS trail.
  2. Payments captures the amount; status updates live from the webhook.
  3. If the final fare is more than 20% above the quote, the reason is shown.

What is on the screen

  • Arrival confirmation
  • Fare breakdown
  • Payment status
  • Rate button
  • Invoice and issue links

Edge cases and validation

  • Payment failed: ask to retry or choose another method; booking blocked until paid.
  • Cash ride: shows 'Pay ₹248 to driver'.
  • Toll or parking added by driver: listed with a photo of the receipt.
