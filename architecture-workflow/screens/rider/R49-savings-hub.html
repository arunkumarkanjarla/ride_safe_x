How it works

  1. Budget is a user goal; the app warns before a booking takes them over.
  2. Deals are ranked by the user's history and what is valid near them now.
  3. The pass is a subscription with clear benefits and a break-even hint.
  4. Price alerts watch flexible trips and notify or auto-book.
