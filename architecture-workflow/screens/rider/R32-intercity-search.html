How it works

  1. Origin and destination resolve to cities and nearby boarding points.
  2. Travellers, luggage and the parcel toggle filter options that physically fit.
  3. Budget is a soft limit: options above it are shown but ranked lower.
  4. Search fans out to bus operator APIs, intercity driver supply and rental partners.

What is on the screen

  • Trip type
  • From and to
  • Date and time window
  • Travellers and luggage
  • Optional parcel
  • Priority chips
  • Optional budget

Edge cases and validation

  • Trips longer than 12 hours suggest an overnight stop.
