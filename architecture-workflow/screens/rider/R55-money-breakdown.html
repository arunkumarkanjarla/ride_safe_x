How it works

  1. Opened from the trip receipt (R17), the invoice (R19) or the fare on ride options (R09) before booking.
  2. Lines come from the stored ride breakdown, the same record the driver sees, so both always match.
  3. The app checks that the lines add up to the amount paid and shows the sum as proof.
  4. Explains that the platform is paid by drivers' small daily fee, not by riders and not by a cut of the ride.

What is on the screen

  • Total paid with zone and payment method
  • Colour bar of the split
  • Driver share (100%)
  • GST to government
  • Platform commission ₹0
  • How the app is paid for
  • Sum check
  • Public fares and report links

Edge cases and validation

  • Coupon used: shown as 'Paid by the platform'; the driver still gets the full fare.
  • Airport or toll charge: its own line, 100% passed on with the receipt photo.
  • Card payment: the card fee is paid by the platform; the rider's total and the driver's share are unchanged.
  • Shared seat: shows the rider's own share only.
