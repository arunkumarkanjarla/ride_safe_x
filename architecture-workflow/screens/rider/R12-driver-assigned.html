How it works

  1. Driver position arrives over WebSocket every 4 s and animates smoothly.
  2. ETA is recalculated every 30 s.
  3. OTP must be told to the driver; the trip can't start without it.
  4. Call and chat use masked channels.

What is on the screen

  • Live car on map with route to pickup
  • ETA and distance
  • Trip OTP
  • Driver photo, name, rating
  • Car model and plate
  • Call, chat, share, safety
  • Cancel

Data used

  • Driver public profile
  • Vehicle

Edge cases and validation

  • Cancel after 3 minutes: shows the cancellation fee before confirming.
  • Driver not moving for 5 minutes: offer to find another driver.
  • Plate shown large so riders can check the car before getting in.
