How it works

  1. Messages go over the ride WebSocket and are stored by the Support service.
  2. Quick replies are pre-translated, so a rider and driver who speak different languages still understand each other.
  3. Driver sees messages read aloud while driving.

What is on the screen

  • Driver header with call button
  • Retention notice
  • Message bubbles
  • Quick replies
  • Message box

Edge cases and validation

  • Phone numbers, links and abusive words are masked and can trigger a safety review.
  • Chat closes 30 minutes after the trip.
