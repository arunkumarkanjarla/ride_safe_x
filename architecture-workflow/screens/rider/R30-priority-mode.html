Choose your priority

Lets the user say what they care about today: speed, price, time flexibility or deals. Ranking follows; choices stay open.

How it works

  1. The choice sets weights for time, price, comfort and walking used by the ranking service.
  2. Saver turns on shared rides, bus legs, batched delivery and off-peak suggestions.
  3. Urgent turns on priority dispatch and hides options that need waiting or transfers at the top of the list.
  4. The choice can be remembered per trip type (city, long trip, food).

Edge cases and validation

  • Medical emergencies go to Emergency mode, not Urgent, so there is no surge or upsell.
  • Accessibility needs (wheelchair, assistance) apply in every mode.
