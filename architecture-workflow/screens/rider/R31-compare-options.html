How it works

  1. The journey service asks rides, sharing and public transport adapters in parallel (under 1.5 s).
  2. Each option gets a score from time, price, transfers and comfort weighted by the priority.
  3. Badges are computed, not paid: Cheapest, Fastest, Eco, Best value.
  4. Add-ons are opt-in chips and change the price live.

What is on the screen

  • Route and time
  • Priority chips
  • Options with time, transfers and price
  • Badges
  • Optional add-ons
  • Book button

Edge cases and validation

  • Public transport shown only where schedule data exists for the city.
  • Shared ride can fail to match: the rider is offered a solo ride at the shared price difference or a refund.
