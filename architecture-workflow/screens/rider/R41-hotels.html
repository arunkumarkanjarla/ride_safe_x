How it works

  1. Inventory comes from hotel partners directly or through channel managers.
  2. Price band is set from the nightly rate compared with the city's spread.
  3. Distance is measured from the trip's arrival point, not the city centre.
  4. Bundle discounts apply when booked with transport in the same plan.

What is on the screen

  • Dates and guests
  • Price band filter
  • Location filter
  • Hotel cards with tier, distance, rating, policy, price
  • Bundle offer
